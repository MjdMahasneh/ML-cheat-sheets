# LLMs Cheatsheet

---

## 1. Tokenization
- Byte-Pair Encoding (BPE), WordPiece.
- Map text → token IDs.

---

## 2. Architecture
- Transformer-based: layers of self-attention + feedforward.
- Context window: sequence length limit.

---

## 3. Training
- Pretrain: masked language modeling, next-token prediction.
- Fine-tune: supervised on downstream tasks.

---

## 4. Inference
- Greedy, beam search, sampling (top-k, top-p).
- Temperature scaling.

---

## 5. Evaluation
- Perplexity, BLEU, ROUGE, human evaluation.

---
