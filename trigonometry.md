# Trigonometry Cheatsheet

---

## 1. Basic Definitions

**Sine:** <img src="https://latex.codecogs.com/png.latex?\sin(\theta)=\frac{\text{opposite}}{\text{hypotenuse}}" alt="sin(theta) = opposite/hypotenuse" />  
Opposite side over hypotenuse.  
- Example: <img src="https://latex.codecogs.com/png.latex?\sin(30^\circ)=0.5" alt="sin(30°)=0.5" />

---

**Cosine:** <img src="https://latex.codecogs.com/png.latex?\cos(\theta)=\frac{\text{adjacent}}{\text{hypotenuse}}" alt="cos(theta) = adjacent/hypotenuse" />  
Adjacent side over hypotenuse.  
- Example: <img src="https://latex.codecogs.com/png.latex?\cos(60^\circ)=0.5" alt="cos(60°)=0.5" />

---
**Tangent:** <img src="https://latex.codecogs.com/png.latex?\tan(\theta)=\frac{\sin(\theta)}{\cos(\theta)}" alt="tan(theta) = sin(theta)/cos(theta)" />

Opposite over adjacent.  

- Example: <img src="https://latex.codecogs.com/png.latex?\tan(45^\circ)=1" alt="tan(45°)=1" />

---

## 2. Pythagorean Identity

**Identity:** <img src="https://latex.codecogs.com/png.latex?\sin^2(\theta)+\cos^2(\theta)=1" alt="sin²θ + cos²θ = 1" />  
Relates sine and cosine for any angle.  
- Example: <img src="https://latex.codecogs.com/png.latex?\sin^2(30^\circ)+\cos^2(30^\circ)=0.25+0.75=1" alt="sin²30°+cos²30°=1" />

---

## 3. Angle Addition & Subtraction

**Sine Addition:** <img src="https://latex.codecogs.com/png.latex?\sin(\alpha+\beta)=\sin\alpha\cos\beta+\cos\alpha\sin\beta" alt="sin(a+b)=sin a cos b + cos a sin b" />  
- Example: <img src="https://latex.codecogs.com/png.latex?\sin(30^\circ+45^\circ)=\sin30^\circ\cos45^\circ+\cos30^\circ\sin45^\circ=0.5\cdot\frac{\sqrt2}{2}+\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\cdot\frac{\sqrt2}{2}" alt="example sin addition" />

---

**Cosine Addition:** <img src="https://latex.codecogs.com/png.latex?\cos(\alpha+\beta)=\cos\alpha\cos\beta-\sin\alpha\sin\beta" alt="cos(a+b)=cos a cos b - sin a sin b" />  
- Example: <img src="https://latex.codecogs.com/png.latex?\cos(30^\circ+45^\circ)=\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\cdot\frac{\sqrt2}{2}-0.5\cdot\frac{\sqrt2}{2}" alt="example cos addition" />

---

**Tangent Addition:** <img src="https://latex.codecogs.com/png.latex?\tan(\alpha+\beta)=\frac{\tan\alpha+\tan\beta}{1-\tan\alpha\tan\beta}" alt="tan(a+b)=(tan a + tan b)/(1 - tan a tan b)" />  
- Example: <img src="https://latex.codecogs.com/png.latex?\tan(30^\circ+45^\circ)=\frac{\frac{1}{\sqrt3}+1}{1-\frac{1}{\sqrt3}\cdot1}" alt="example tan addition" />

---

## 4. Double‑Angle Formulas

**Sine:** <img src="https://latex.codecogs.com/png.latex?\sin(2\theta)=2\sin\theta\cos\theta" alt="sin2θ = 2 sin θ cos θ" />  
- Example: <img src="https://latex.codecogs.com/png.latex?\sin(60^\circ)=2\sin30^\circ\cos30^\circ=2\cdot0.5\cdot\frac{\sqrt3}{2}=\frac{\sqrt3}{2}" alt="example sin double-angle" />

---

**Cosine:** <img src="https://latex.codecogs.com/png.latex?\cos(2\theta)=\cos^2\theta-\sin^2\theta" alt="cos2θ = cos²θ - sin²θ" />  
- Example: <img src="https://latex.codecogs.com/png.latex?\cos(60^\circ)=\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^2-\left(\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\right)^2=\frac{1}{4}-\frac{3}{4}=-\frac{1}{2}" alt="example cos double-angle" />

---

## 5. Derivatives & Integrals

**d/dθ sin θ:** <img src="https://latex.codecogs.com/png.latex?\frac{d}{d\theta}\sin\theta=\cos\theta" alt="d/dθ sin θ = cos θ" />  
- Example: <img src="https://latex.codecogs.com/png.latex?\frac{d}{d\theta}\sin(90^\circ)=\cos(90^\circ)=0" alt="derivative example" />

---

**d/dθ cos θ:** <img src="https://latex.codecogs.com/png.latex?\frac{d}{d\theta}\cos\theta=-\sin\theta" alt="d/dθ cos θ = -sin θ" />  
- Example: <img src="https://latex.codecogs.com/png.latex?\frac{d}{d\theta}\cos(0)=0" alt="derivative example" />

---

**∫ sin θ dθ:** <img src="https://latex.codecogs.com/png.latex?\int\sin\theta\,d\theta=-\cos\theta+C" alt="∫ sin θ dθ = -cos θ + C" />  
- Example: <img src="https://latex.codecogs.com/png.latex?\int_0^\pi\sin\theta\,d\theta=2" alt="integral example" />

---

**∫ cos θ dθ:** <img src="https://latex.codecogs.com/png.latex?\int\cos\theta\,d\theta=\sin\theta+C" alt="∫ cos θ dθ = sin θ + C" />  
- Example: <img src="https://latex.codecogs.com/png.latex?\int_0^{\pi/2}\cos\theta\,d\theta=1" alt="integral example" />
